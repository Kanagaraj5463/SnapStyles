Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 23, 2026

SnapStyles uses location and content you choose from your photo library to provide its core features. The current app does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SnapStyles ("SnapStyles," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the SnapStyles mobile application. It applies to the iOS app identified below and related support communications.

2. Information the app handles

  • Location information. With your permission, SnapStyles accesses your device's precise location while you use the app. It uses this information to center the map, identify your general area, calculate a search radius, and show nearby collaboration opportunities.
  • Photos you select. You may choose a profile photo through Apple's system photo picker. SnapStyles receives only the item you select, not unrestricted access to your photo library. In the current version, the selected image remains in the app session and is not uploaded by us.
  • Preferences and app state. The app handles your selected role, search radius, collaboration preferences, and a device-local flag indicating that you completed the welcome screen. In the current version, these details are not sent to a SnapStyles server.
  • Communications. If you contact support, we receive the information in your message, such as your email address and any details you choose to provide.

3. How we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide maps, nearby discovery, profile, and collaboration features;
  • remember basic app state on your device;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests;
  • maintain security, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable law; and
  • improve the reliability and functionality of SnapStyles.

4. Device permissions and your choices

iOS controls access to location and photos. You may decline a permission or change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security on your device. Location-based features may not work if location access is disabled. SnapStyles does not require continuous background location for its current core experience.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. SnapStyles uses Apple platform services, including iOS location services, Apple Maps, reverse geocoding, and the system photo picker. Apple may process technical or location information when providing those services under Apple's Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards. If a future version lets you publish a profile or collaboration request, information you intentionally publish may be visible to other users as described in that version of the app and this policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Device-local app data is retained on your device until the app clears it or you remove the app. Support communications are retained only as long as needed to answer the request, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. To request access to or deletion of information you sent to us, email the address in the Contact section.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

SnapStyles is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. International use

If you use SnapStyles outside India, information you send to support may be processed in India or other locations where our service providers operate. Data protection laws in those locations may differ from those in your country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SnapStyles changes or as required by law. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or through another appropriate channel.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact SnapStyles at snapstyles@gmail.com.
